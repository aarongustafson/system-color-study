Background and foreground occupy two rows; each capture’s contrast cell spans both. Contrast includes alpha over that capture’s recorded backdrop. Normal text: AA ≥ 4.5:1, AAA ≥ 7:1. Large text: AA ≥ 3:1, AAA ≥ 4.5:1. Grades use unrounded ratios. These are threshold results, not WCAG conformance verdicts.
